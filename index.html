The Proprietor

I'm an alchemist of the optical profession, with more than ten years of experience. Outside of it, I make fiction, original music and original games and graphic designs.

My music is released as a verified independent artist across multiple platforms such as Spotify, iTunes, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Pandora and more. One of my songs is featured on Welcome to Night Vale.

My games are built to run in the browser on any device that can run a web browser, and I'm developing a desktop game in C# as well.

My fiction builds connect worlds across multiple projects. I'm working on multiple books that I'm more than excited about. My writing style is atmospheric paranormal thrillers, fun middle-grade fantasy concepts, and abstract/psychological.
